Help

Simple guidance for using MediReminder and understanding its limits.

How do I use my reminders?

  1. Open Reminders and add a name and daily time.
  2. Open Schedule to find Upcoming, Due and Taken occurrences.
  3. Choose Mark as taken to record an occurrence.
  4. Open History to review the recorded entry.

Why might a reminder not appear?

Keep the page open and the device active. Browser support varies.

Read alert guidance

Where are my records stored?

Records stay in this browser. Clearing browser data can remove them.

For medicine questions, follow your existing care instructions.

Alert and browser guidance

  • The prototype shows preset reminder states.
  • A working application needs permission for browser notifications.
  • Closed pages and sleeping devices cannot guarantee reminders.
  • In-page reminders should remain available if permission is denied.
  • This screen does not request real notification permission.
